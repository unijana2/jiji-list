GIGI LIST V7 — PRIVATE EDITION
by unijana
==============================

ABOUT
GIGI LIST is a personal, retro-inspired music player with a lavender and pink iPod interface. This edition plays your music files locally.

HOW TO USE
1. Keep index.html and the music folder in the same directory.
2. Do not change the names of audio files inside music.
3. Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, or another modern browser.
4. Choose a song in My Library or press Play on the iPod.
5. Shuffle is enabled by default.
6. The heart next to a song (or in the iPod wheel) adds it to Favorites.
7. Dreamy Nights and Love Letters are mood collections.
8. On Repeat shows tracks selected for playback at least twice.
9. Use the search field to find a track.

FOLDER STRUCTURE
gigi-list-v6-private/
  index.html
  README.txt
  music/
    [your audio files]

IMPORTANT
- The music folder is required for the bundled songs to play.
- Keep the original filenames and folder structure.
- This is the PRIVATE edition. Do not publish or upload commercial music files to a public GitHub repository or hosting service without appropriate distribution rights.
- Favorites and playback counts are stored in this browser's local storage; they are not synced between devices and may be lost when browser data is cleared.
- If you share only index.html, other people will see the interface but cannot play your local audio files.
- You can use the Import Music button to add files from your device during a session.

FEATURED TRACK
Vacay To Bonetown — Gigi's favorite ♡

DESIGN
Retro iPod, lavender / pink, subtle unicorn icon, "by unijana" signature.
On mobile, the iPod appears before the main track list.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- No audio: check that music/ is beside index.html, and that the files match their original names.
- Try opening the site from a normal local folder rather than a compressed ZIP preview.
- If a file fails in one browser, try Chrome or Safari.
- Refresh the page if the controls stop responding.
